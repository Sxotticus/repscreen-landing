How it works

Simple. No excuses.

RepScreen uses your camera and Apple's Family Controls API to verify real reps before unlocking your phone.

1

Set your rep goal

Choose your exercise — push-ups, squats, planks, jumping jacks, or create your own custom movement — and how many reps unlock your phone each day.

2

Phone locks until you move

Apple Family Controls blocks access to all apps. Your camera detects reps in real time using on-device ML — no internet needed.

3

Complete your reps, unlock your day

Hit your goal and the phone opens. Every single day. No workarounds, no skipping, no negotiating with yourself.

4

Track streaks & set family profiles

Monitor progress across your whole family. Parents set goals for kids. Kids earn their screen time with real effort.
